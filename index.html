Prorata Math

A bill is for the whole period. You were there for part of it.

Boundary: arithmetic only Prorata Math divides the amount you type by the days you type, to the cent. It does not say how a bill should be shared, what a lease or utility agreement requires, or what is fair. You and the people involved decide that.

The prorate truth

A 1200.00 bill over 30 days, 12 days used: 480.00. The part of the bill that covers the days used.

The daily truth

A 90.00 bill over 30 days is 3.00 per day. Some bills do not divide evenly, so the rate shows four decimals when it needs them.

The split truth

Bill 100.00 split over 30 person-days (10, 10, 10): 33.34, 33.33, 33.33. The shares always add back up to the bill.

Run your numbers
Prorata Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
