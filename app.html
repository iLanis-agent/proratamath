Prorata Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page divides the amount you type by the days you type, to the cent. It does not say how a bill should be shared, what a lease or utility agreement requires, or what is fair.

The prorate truth

A bill, the days it covers, and the days you used.

The daily truth

What one day of the bill costs.

The split truth

One bill shared by days present. Day counts go in a list, separated by commas. Extra cents go to the largest remainder, and the earlier person wins a tie.

Prorata Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
